## Brand & Style
This design system defines an authoritative, refined digital curriculum vitae and academic/editorial portfolio for Bau Nuraisyah Parenrengi .M, bridging Islamic Economic Jurisprudence (Hukum Ekonomi Syariah) and contemplative creative writing.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Scholarly & Jurisprudential:** Structured, dignified, and trustworthy, projecting deep analytical acumen in Islamic commercial law, governance, and ethical finance.
- **Literary & Poetic:** Subtle literary warmth, evoking editorial depth, intellectual rigor, and quiet sophistication.
- **Poised & Modern:** Free of ornamental excess; uses precise alignment, organic warm negative space, and disciplined typographic hierarchy to convey contemporary professional maturity.

### Design Style
The system pairs **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Foundations**. Rather than sterile tech minimalism or ornate traditional tropes, it utilizes rich parchment-inspired surfaces (`#fcfbf7`), deep evergreen anchors (`#065f46`), hairline architectural dividers, and restrained champagne gilded notes. Visual interest relies on contrast between high-contrast serif editorial titles, crisp geometric metadata, and tactile hairline containers.

## Colors
The color palette represents integrity, academic honor, and ethical stewardship through deep emerald greens balanced against warm ivory foundations and muted champagne accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#065f46` - Deep Emerald):** The foundational anchor for primary actions, prominent headlines, identity branding, and key timeline indicators. Emits intellectual gravitas and syariah fidelity.
- **Secondary (`#c59f60` - Muted Champagne Gold):** Used sparingly for prestige accents, academic distinction tags, honors, active pill highlights, and refined visual markers.
- **Tertiary (`#047857` - Syariah Green):** An interactive companion to the primary tone; powers interactive states, active tab lines, link focus states, and secondary status indicators.
- **Neutral (`#334155` - Deep Slate Body):** The calibrated reading tone that prevents eye fatigue while providing high legibility against cream backgrounds.

### Extended Palette & Surface Tokens
- **Canvas Base:** `#fcfbf7` (Warm Ivory / Raw Linen)
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card Backgrounds):** `#f7f5ee` (Subtle Cream)
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Elevated Sheets / Popovers):** `#ffffff` (Pure White contrast)
- **Border / Hairlines:** `#e2e8f0` (Slate Hairline) with contextual `#e8e4d9` (Parchment Border)
- **Muted Text / Metadata:** `#64748b` (Refined Slate)
- **High-Contrast Text:** `#0f172a` (Obsidian Slate for titles and author name)
- **Accent Emerald Glow:** `#10b981` (Vibrant Emerald for live states and publication year badges)

## Typography
The typographic rhythm establishes an intellectual balance between legal scholarship and literary publication:

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys academic heritage, editorial balance, and humanistic warmth. It should be used for the candidate's name, major section titles, thesis titles, and publication headlines. Use italic variations sparingly for literary excerpts and citations.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides neutral, open, and contemporary legibility. Its wide apertures ensure effortless reading for legal case analyses, article abstracts, and curriculum listings across dense viewports.
- **Metadata & Badges:** Utilize `label-md` and `label-sm` set in medium/semibold uppercase tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to delineate categorical taxonomy such as legal sub-disciplines, publication dates, and credentials.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout architecture is structured around an asymmetrical editorial grid that frames academic authority and creative writing side-by-side.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column adaptive fluid grid constrained to a maximum content container of `1280px` for wide screens to preserve ideal reading line-lengths (55–75 characters per line).
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Left column (4 cols) holds fixed candidate identity, biographical anchor, credential highlights, and quick contact; Right column (8 cols) accommodates tabbed portfolios, legal essays, interactive timeline, and writing anthologies.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Collapses into an 8-column flow with persistent top identity summary and stacked sections.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked stream. Margin shifts to `1.25rem`, gutters to `1rem`. Timeline tracks compress into a single vertical baseline along the left edge.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system avoids heavy, dark drop shadows. Instead, it relies on tonal separation, delicate paper stratification, and whisper-thin outlines.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** `#fcfbf7` warm ivory field. Completely flat.
- **Card Surfaces (Level 1):** `#ffffff` or `#f7f5ee` with a 1px border of `#e2e8f0`. Shadow is subtle and tinted: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 6px 16px -4px rgba(6, 95, 70, 0.03)`.
- **Interactive Card Hover (Level 2):** Subtle upward translation (`translateY(-2px)`) accompanied by an expanded ambient ring: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(6, 95, 70, 0.08), 0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)` and border transition to `#cbd5e1`.
- **Overlays / Floating Actions / Modals (Level 3):** Frosted glass combined with deep depth: `background: rgba(252, 251, 247, 0.88)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, bordered by `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`, and shadow `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1)`.

## Shapes
A tailored `Soft (1)` corner geometry is implemented throughout the design system. 

- **Base Components:** 0.25rem (`4px`) on input fields, chips, small badges, and timeline nodes.
- **Cards & Enclosures (`rounded-lg`):** 0.5rem (`8px`) on project cards, article summaries, and modal containers. This maintains crisp, editorial structure while avoiding severe industrial harshness.
- **Specialized Pill Accents:** Fully rounded geometry is reserved exclusively for micro-status indicators (e.g., "Available for Consulting", category tags, or publication date pills) to deliver a clear visual contrast against structured rectangular paper blocks.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Button:** `#065f46` background, `#ffffff` typography, `rounded-md` (0.375rem), padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Smooth transition (`200ms ease`). Hover state triggers a shift to `#047857` with shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(6, 95, 70, 0.2)`. Focus ring is `2px` offset with `#10b981`.
- **Secondary / Outline Button:** `#ffffff` or transparent background, `1px solid #065f46`, text in `#065f46`. Hover state fills the background with `rgba(6, 95, 70, 0.05)`.
- **Literary / Text Action:** Obsidian text `#0f172a` with an underline positioned `4px` below the baseline in `#c59f60` (Champagne Gold), brightening to `#047857` on hover.

### 2. Timeline Components (Academic & Career Path)
- **Spine:** Continuous `1.5px` vertical rule in `#e2e8f0`.
- **Timeline Node:** `12px` circular marker with a `2px` border in `#065f46` over a `#fcfbf7` center. Active or current milestone fills the center with `#c59f60`.
- **Entry Block:** Card containing title (`Playfair Display`), institution/publication name (`Plus Jakarta Sans Semibold`), period badge, and clean descriptive body text.

### 3. Badges & Chips
- **Syariah / Legal Focus Badge:** Background `rgba(6, 95, 70, 0.08)`, text `#065f46`, border `1px solid rgba(6, 95, 70, 0.2)`, `rounded-full`, tracking `0.05em`.
- **Editorial / Literary Badge:** Background `rgba(197, 159, 96, 0.12)`, text `#8a6521`, border `1px solid rgba(197, 159, 96, 0.3)`.

### 4. Cards (Publication & Jurisprudence Dossiers)
- Built on `#ffffff` surface with `1px solid #e2e8f0` and `8px` corner radius.
- Includes a subtle top border highlight: a `2px` decorative strip in alternating `#065f46` (for legal studies) and `#c59f60` (for literature/creative essays).
- Generous internal padding: `space-lg` (`1.5rem`).

### 5. Input Fields & Contact Elements
- Background `#fcfbf7`, border `1px solid #cbd5e1`, font `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
- Focus state: border color switches cleanly to `#065f46` with a non-intrusive `3px` outer aura of `rgba(6, 95, 70, 0.12)`. No abrupt shadows.

### 6. Interactive Writing Excerpt Viewer
- Blockquote styling featuring an oversized quotation glyph in `#c59f60` at 20% opacity.
- Body set in `Playfair Display` regular italic (`1.125rem`, line height `1.85`).
- Attributions anchored by an em-dash in `#64748b` with metadata in `label-sm`.
